1–0–2The game

What is pickleball?

Pickleball is a racket sport played with a solid paddle and a light plastic ball with holes, on a court the size of a badminton doubles court. It’s mostly played as doubles, the serve is underhand, and the rallies are long. Imagine tennis, badminton and table tennis had a very friendly baby.

The whole game in four lines

  1. Serve underhand, diagonally across the court.
  2. Let it bounce once on each side before anyone volleys. That’s the two-bounce rule.
  3. Don’t volley from the kitchen: the 2.13 m (7 ft) zone on each side of the net. Step in any time, but there the ball has to bounce first.
  4. Games go to 11, win by 2. Only the serving side scores.

Badische Küche, now with a net.

The kitchen is the zone right next to the net, 2.13 m (7 ft) deep on each side. You can walk in whenever you like; you just can’t volley the ball while you’re in it.

2–0–2Why play

Why it hooks people.

  • Quick start

    Easy to get into. The serve is underhand and the ball is slow, so real rallies come early.

  • Doubles

    Made for company. It’s mostly doubles on a small court, so you talk, laugh and swap partners.

  • All ages

    Every age, one court. Students, parents and retirees play together.

  • Your pace

    Your pace. The ball is slow and the court is small, so gentle or fast is up to you.

  • Small court

    Fits in a sports hall. The court is the size of a badminton doubles court, so many halls already have the lines.

5–0–2The club

Run by volunteers.

Pickleball Südbaden e.V. is a registered association, run by volunteers. We’re based in Südbaden, in the border region with France and Switzerland, and players from Alsace and Basel are very welcome.

We’re starting small: first halls, first dates, first players. If you’d like to help shape the club, write to us.

6–0–2Questions

Good questions.

Not answered here? Ask us by email.

Do I need any experience?

None at all. The rules fit in four lines, and the first rallies come quickly.

What do I need to bring?

We’ll say what to bring when we post the first dates. In sports halls you always need indoor shoes with light, non-marking soles.

Where and when do you play?

That isn’t fixed yet. We’re looking for our first halls in Südbaden, and dates and places will be posted here first. Know a hall? Tell us.

What does it cost?

Membership details are coming. We’ll publish them here.

Which languages do you speak?

This website is in German and English, and French is coming. You can write to us in German or English.

Is there an age limit?

Pickleball works at almost any age, and students, parents and retirees often play together on one court. When we post dates, we’ll say who each one suits.
